Expert review · Phase 4

How experts will score the model

A panel of experts scores each part of the model and writes comments.

  • Relevant
  • Clear
  • Workable
  • Complete
  • Suitable
I-CVIscore for each item
S-CVI/Aveaverage for the whole model

Small trial

What the trial checks

After the expert review, we try parts of the model on a small scale. The focus is on how agencies refer people to each other.

  • Does it work
  • Do people accept it
  • Are roles clear
  • What gets in the way
  • What to improve
